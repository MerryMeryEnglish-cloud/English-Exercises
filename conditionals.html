How Conditionals Work

Conditionals consist of an If Clause (condition) and a Main/Cause Clause (result). The order can be reversed without changing the meaning!

Zero Conditional

General truths, scientific facts & habits.

If + Present Simple, ... Present Simple
If you heat water to 100°C, it boils.

First Conditional

Real or likely future situations.

If + Present Simple, ... Will + Verb
If it rains tomorrow, we will stay at home.

Second Conditional

Hypothetical or unlikely present/future situations.

If + Past Simple, ... Would + Verb
If I won the lottery, I would buy a house.

Third Conditional

Imaginary past situations & regrets.

If + Past Perfect, ... Would have + Past Participle
If she had studied, she would have passed.
