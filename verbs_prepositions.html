WITH
  • appointment with: She booked an appointment with the doctor.
  • argument with*: He had an argument with his colleague.
  • contact with: I recently lost contact with an old friend.
  • difficulty with: She had difficulty with the software.
  • matter with: What is the matter with this machine?
  • relationship with: He maintains a great relationship with clients.
  • trouble with: We ran into unexpected trouble with the engine.
FOR
  • cure for: Scientists seek a cure for the illness.
  • demand for: There is high demand for skilled technicians.
  • need for: There is a clear need for swift action.
  • recipe for: Good sleep is a recipe for high focus.
  • respect for: Students showed respect for their teacher.
  • room for: There is always room for improvement.
  • use for: I have no further use for this old manual.
IN
  • belief in: Her belief in justice never wavered.
  • change in: We noticed a sudden change in temperature.
  • decrease in: There was a sharp decrease in complaints.
  • increase in: The firm recorded an increase in profits.
  • interest in: She developed an interest in astrophysics.
  • rise in: We observed a steady rise in fuel costs.
  • success in: His dedication brought success in his career.
ON
  • agreement on: Both ministers signed an agreement on trade.
  • article on*: Did you read her article on AI?
  • ban on: The city council placed a ban on plastic bags.
  • debate on: They initiated a debate on educational reforms.
  • decision on: We will announce our decision on the merger.
  • information on: Please send me more information on this trip.
  • report on: I drafted an extensive report on productivity.
TO
  • addiction to: Overcoming an addiction to sugar is tough.
  • attention to: Pay close attention to subtle details.
  • invitation to: We received a formal invitation to the ball.
  • promise to: Keep your promise to help the community.
  • reaction to: His reaction to the test result was pure relief.
  • solution to: They formulated a solution to the crisis.
  • threat to: Wildfires pose an immense threat to forests.

  • advantage of: Take advantage of these learning resources.
  • cause of: What was the real cause of the outage?
  • chance of: Is there any chance of rain tonight?
  • cost of: The cost of living varies by country.
  • lack of: The project stalled due to lack of time.
  • possibility of: There is a strong possibility of success.
  • photo of: She framed a scenic photo of the mountains.

  • discussion about: We wrapped up our discussion about goals.
  • story about: Grandfather told us a vivid story about his youth.
