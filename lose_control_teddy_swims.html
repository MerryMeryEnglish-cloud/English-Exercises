Vocal & Pronunciation Tips
  • Connected Speech: Notice how individual words blend smoothly together in fast singing:
    "got a hold of me" → "got-a-hold-a-me".
  • Emotion & Intonation: Teddy Swims uses a raspy belt in the chorus. Pay attention to how he projects emotional power on "falling apart".
  • Rhythm & Shadowing: Practice shadowing (singing along simultaneously with the track) to match natural native speech pace and rhythm.

About Teddy Swims

Real Name: Jaten Dimsdale

Hometown: Atlanta, Georgia, USA

Genres: Soul, R&B, Country, Pop, Rock

Jaten Dimsdale (Teddy Swims)

Global Soul & R&B Sensation

Teddy Swims is an American singer-songwriter famous for blending soul, R&B, country, and rock with an intensely emotional, raspy vocal range.

Career Milestones & Breakthrough
  • What does SWIMS mean? "SWIMS" is an acronym for "Someone Who Isn't Me Sometimes", representing his philosophy that performing allows him to express parts of himself he otherwise couldn't.
  • YouTube Beginnings: He initially gained global attention by posting soulful cover songs from his home studio, covering artists ranging from Shania Twain to Amy Winehouse.
  • Global Chart-Topper: His hit single "Lose Control" reached #1 on the US Billboard Hot 100 and topped music charts across Europe, Australia, and New Zealand.
  • Debut Album: The track is the lead single from his debut studio album, I've Tried Everything But Therapy (Part 1), released in 2023 to critical acclaim.
