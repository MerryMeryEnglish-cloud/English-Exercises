📌 Affirmative, Negative & Questions

Affirmative No Do / Does Needed

Add -s / -es to the main verb with He, She, It. Keep the base verb with I, You, We, They.

She works from 9 to 5.

I do exercise every day.

Negative Doesn't / Don't

Use doesn't (he/she/it) or don't (I/you/we/they) + the base verb (no -s on the verb).

He doesn't like heavy metal.

They don't eat meat.

Questions Does / Do + Subject

Place Does or Do before the subject, followed by the base verb.

Does your mother know?

Do you like dancing?

💬 Short Answers & Full Answers

Questions with DOES He / She / It
Does your mother know?
Short Yes, she does. / No, she doesn't.
Full Yes, she knows. / No, she doesn't know.
Does the train arrive on time?
Short Yes, it does. / No, it doesn't.
Full Yes, it arrives on time. / No, it doesn't arrive on time.
Questions with DO I / You / We / They
Do you like dancing?
Short Yes, I do. / No, I don't.
Full Yes, I like dancing. / No, I don't like dancing.
Do they live near the park?
Short Yes, they do. / No, they don't.
Full Yes, they live near the park. / No, they don't live near the park.
