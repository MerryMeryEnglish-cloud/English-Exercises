Verbs with Infinitive or Gerund in British English

Master when to use the -ing form (gerund) vs the to + infinitive.

Dictionary

When to use Gerunds (-ing)

  • After prepositions: e.g., interested in learning.
  • As subjects of sentences: e.g., Swimming is healthy.
  • After specific verbs: e.g., enjoy, avoid, mind, finish.

When to use Infinitives (to + verb)

  • To express purpose/reason: e.g., I came to help.
  • After adjectives: e.g., happy to meet.
  • After specific verbs: e.g., want, decide, hope, refuse.
