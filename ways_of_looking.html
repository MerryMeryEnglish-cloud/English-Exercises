LOOK vs SEE vs WATCH

The foundation of visual verbs

LOOK
👁️ To direct your eyes.
Intentional & conscious action.
  • Look at that cute bird!
  • Look both ways before crossing.
SEE
💡 To perceive naturally.
Happens without conscious effort.
  • I see a car outside.
  • Did you see him at the party?
WATCH
⏱️ To observe over time.
Focused on movement or activity.
  • We watched a movie last night.
  • He is watching his little brother.
