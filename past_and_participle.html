Pedagogical Guide & Grammar Scope

Designed for A2+ adult learners preparing for real-world travel communication:

  • Past Simple: Used for completed past events at a specific time (flew, bought, took, arrived). In questions: Did + subject + base form (e.g., Did you buy...?).
  • Past Participle: Used with auxiliary verbs for experiences and completed preparations (Have you ever visited...?, had already boarded...).
  • Automatic Instant Validation: No "Check" button needed. Immediate sound and visual cues confirm every single action.
  • Audio Controls: Dedicated Play, Pause, and Resume buttons provide native listening practice.
