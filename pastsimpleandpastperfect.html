📘 B2 Grammar Summary: Past Simple vs. Past Perfect

Master the chronological sequence of events without ambiguity.

💡 Answering tip: Contractions are fully accepted (e.g., hadn't or had not, 'd left or had left). When an auxiliary verb is unnecessary or redundant in Spot & Fix, you can simply type a hyphen (-) to remove it.

Past Simple (V2 / didn't + V1)

Single completed actions, chronological narratives, or immediate consecutive events. Notice invariant verbs like hit, cut, set, cost do not change form in past simple.

"The car hit the barrier and came to an immediate stop."
"She shut the door quietly and left the keys behind."

Past Perfect (had + V3)

Actions completed before another past event or point in time (the earlier past). Watch for irregular participles and invariant verbs (had hit, had torn, had frozen).

"By the time help arrived, the storm had torn the roof off."
"The pipe burst because the water inside had frozen overnight."
