Simple Past vs. Present Perfect

Understand when to look backward at finished history versus connecting past actions with irregular forms to our present moment.

Conceptual Time Axis

SP

Simple Past

Structure:

Simple past (regular & irregular verbs)

  • Use: Finished actions at a specific past time.
  • Example: "We booked our flights / We went."
PP

Present Perfect

Structure:

Have / has + verb in perfect / 3rd column

  • Use: Experiences & past actions with connection to now.
  • Example with Irregular Verb: "I have drunk fresh coconut water." (drink → drank → drunk)
Reading Practice

Vacaciones en Brasil

Last month, my family and I decided to take an unforgettable trip to Brazil. We booked our flights in advance and packed our bags with excitement. I have always wanted to visit South America, so this adventure was a dream come true for me.

We arrived in Rio de Janeiro on a sunny Monday morning. Since then, we have explored many iconic places. Yesterday, we climbed the famous Sugarloaf Mountain and enjoyed a breathtaking panoramic view of the coastline. I have tasted delicious local dishes like feijoada and have drunk fresh coconut water on Copacabana Beach.

So far, we have visited three different cities and met wonderful, friendly people. Although our vacation is almost over, I know I will never forget these magical moments. Traveling has taught me so much about new cultures!
