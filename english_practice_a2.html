Key Vocabulary & Expressions

make / earn / win / spend money

We make/earn a salary, win a prize, and spend money.

have been (for/since) / have known

They've been together for years. Known him since 2020.

didn't use to / used to

I didn't use to wear a watch, but I used to wear rings.

depends on / worry about / agree on

It depends on the time. Don't worry about it.

one-way / round-trip / return ticket

I'd like a return ticket. Do you have direct flights?

departure / arrival / boarding time

What is the departure time? When are we boarding?

reservation / book a room / check-out

I have a reservation. What time is check-out?

ask for the bill / pay in cash / tip

Could we have the bill? Can I pay by card?
