🛫 Metro Airport: Hand Luggage & Cabin Rules

Level A2 • Travel

What items can passengers take on board as hand luggage?

Please remember that each passenger is allowed to carry ONE cabin suitcase into the aircraft. This suitcase must not exceed the dimensions of 55cm x 22cm x 35cm and must not weigh more than 10kg.

You can also carry one small personal accessory, such as a laptop bag, small rucksack, or handbag, which must fit easily under the seat in front of you. If you bring both a suitcase and a handbag, their combined total weight cannot exceed 10kg.

If your luggage is too bulky or exceeds the weight limit, you will not be permitted to take it into the cabin. Airline staff will transfer your bag into the hold (the aircraft luggage compartment), and an extra handling fee will be charged.

⚠️ Essential Security Regulations:

  • Electronic Devices: Make sure your mobile phones, laptops, and tablets are fully charged before reaching the checkpoint so that security staff can turn them on and inspect them.
  • Liquids & Gels: Liquids brought from home in containers larger than 100ml are strictly forbidden. However, bottles over 100ml are allowed on board if they are bought at airport shops after passing security screening.

Have a safe journey and enjoy your flight!
