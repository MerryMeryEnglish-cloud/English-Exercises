About Maroon 5 & Adam Levine

Maroon 5 is an American pop rock band from Los Angeles, California. The group originated in 1994 as Kara's Flowers while attending high school, consisting of lead vocalist Adam Levine, keyboardist Jesse Carmichael, bassist Mickey Madden, and drummer Ryan Dusick.

In 2001, guitarist James Valentine joined the group, prompting their official rebirth as Maroon 5. Their breakthrough album, Songs About Jane (2002), generated global smash hits like "This Love" and "She Will Be Loved", earning the band the Grammy Award for Best New Artist. Later albums produced monumental chart records including "Makes Me Wonder", "Moves Like Jagger", "One More Night", "Sugar", "Girls Like You", and "Animals".

⭐ Adam Levine's Solo Career & Collaborations: Outside of Maroon 5, frontman Adam Levine has established a notable solo musical footprint. He starred in the musical film Begin Again (2013) and performed its Oscar-nominated solo soundtrack single "Lost Stars". He has also scored major worldwide solo hits collaborating with other prominent artists, including Gym Class Heroes on "Stereo Hearts", R. City on "Locked Away", 50 Cent and Eminem on "My Life", and Jason Derulo on "Lifestyle". In addition, Levine contributed solo tracks to blockbuster films such as "Good Mood" for PAW Patrol: The Movie and launched his own record imprint, 222 Records.
