Verb "Get" Masterclass & Audio Pronunciation

The verb get is one of the most versatile words in English. Click on the speaker icon 🔊 next to any sample sentence to listen to native pronunciation.

1. Receive / Obtain / Fetch

  • I got an unexpected email from my former manager today.
  • Could you get me those financial reports from the printer?

2. Become / Change of State

  • As evening approached, it began to get much colder.
  • She was thrilled when she got promoted last month.

3. Arrive / Reach / Transport

  • What time do you usually get to the office in morning traffic?
  • We finally got home after a long eight-hour flight.
  • Can you get me to the train station before 5 PM?
    💡 Note: get someone to a place = llevar/trasladar a alguien a un lugar (ej: "¿Puedes llevarme a la estación de tren?").

4. Key Phrasal Verbs

  • get through = communicate by phone / complete a task
    💡 Note: get through = lograr comunicarse por teléfono. En cambio, put someone through = transferir/comunicar a otra persona por teléfono (ej: "I will put you through to Mr. Smith.").
  • get to (someone) = affect or annoy someone emotionally
  • get over = recover from illness, breakup, or shock
  • get away with = escape punishment for something
  • get by = manage to survive financially or with available resources
  • get down to = start focusing seriously on a task
