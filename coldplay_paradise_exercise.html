Origins & Global Impact

Coldplay are a British rock band formed in London in 1997. The group consists of vocalist and pianist Chris Martin, guitarist Jonny Buckland, bassist Guy Berryman, and drummer and percussionist Will Champion, alongside manager Phil Harvey.

After meeting at University College London, they achieved worldwide fame with their 2000 debut album Parachutes and the breakthrough single "Yellow". Over the past two decades, Coldplay have become one of the best-selling music acts of all time, known for stadium anthems, atmospheric piano melodies, and emotionally resonant storytelling.

Released in September 2011 as the second single from their fifth studio album Mylo Xyloto, "Paradise" topped charts across Europe and earned a Grammy nomination for Best Pop Duo/Group Performance.
