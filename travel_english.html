💡 Key Travel Vocabulary (Level B1): Review these common travel expressions before completing the exercises.
Boarding pass Noun

Your travel document (paper or phone) indicating your gate and seat number.

"Please show your boarding pass at the gate."
Carry-on luggage Noun

Small bag or small suitcase allowed with you in the aircraft cabin.

"My carry-on luggage fits easily in the overhead compartment."
Book in advance Collocation

To reserve a ticket or room earlier than your travel date.

"We booked our museum tickets in advance to skip the line."
Delay / Delayed Verb / Adj

When transport departs or arrives later than the scheduled time.

"Our train was delayed by forty minutes."
Check-in desk Noun

The airport counter where baggage is dropped off and documents checked.

"Let's go straight to the check-in desk."
Shuttle bus Noun

A regular vehicle transporting travelers between the airport and hotel/city.

"The hotel shuttle bus leaves every thirty minutes."
Customs Noun

The border authority that checks goods entering or leaving a country.

"We went through customs after collecting our suitcases."
Direct flight Noun

A journey by air without intermediate stops or plane changes.

"We prefer a direct flight to avoid waiting at connections."
