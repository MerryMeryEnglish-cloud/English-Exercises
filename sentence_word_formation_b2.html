📚 Overview & Master Grammar Summary (B2/B2+)

Welcome to the Sentence & Word Formation Masterclass. This module trains your grammatical precision across key Cambridge B2 First (FCE) and B2+ syllabus areas: complex mixed and third conditionals, reported speech verb structures, passive voice tenses, habit structures (used to / be used to / get used to), question tags, compound adjectives, prepositions, phrasal verbs, and derivational word formation.

1. Conditionals (Mixed & 3rd)

Combine past unreal conditions with present consequences, or explore hypothetical past scenarios.

"If you had taken that post, would you be living in London today?"

2. Passive Voice in Complex Tenses

Continuous passives (is being restored) and modal perfect passives (should have been delivered).

"The historical manuscript is currently being restored by specialists."

3. Habits: Used to vs Be/Get used to

Used to + base verb (past habits) vs be/get used to + -ing (accustomed to a routine).

"Did it take you long to get used to commuting by train every morning?"

4. Reported Speech & Advanced Verbs

Reporting verbs with specific patterns (admit + -ing, persuade someone + to-infinitive, apologize for + -ing).

"The manager persuaded her to reconsider her sudden resignation."

5. Question Tags & Phrasal Verbs

Subtle question tags (Let's → shall we?, negative adverbs) and multi-word phrasal verbs.

"You could hardly hear the audio from the back row, could you?"

6. Derivational Word Formation

Transforming root words into nouns (-ship, -ence, -ity), adjectives (-able, -ive), negative forms (un-, dis-), or verbs (en-, -ize).

"LEAD → Her exceptional leadership skills inspired the entire research department."
