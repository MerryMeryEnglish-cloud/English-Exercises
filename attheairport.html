Essential Travel & Airport Phrases

Listen to each phrase carefully with British pronunciation and understand how to use them during your trip.

1. "What is the purpose of your visit?"

Asked by immigration officers to know if you are traveling for tourism, business, or studies.

2. "I am here for tourism / vacation."

The standard direct response to immigration when visiting for leisure.

3. "How long are you planning to stay?"

Question regarding the duration of your stay in the destination country.

4. "I am staying for two weeks."

Clear declaration of duration using the present continuous or correct tense structure.

5. "Where will you be staying?"

Inquiry about your accommodation (hotel name, address, or friend's house).

6. "Could I have a window seat, please?"

Polite request at check-in counter to sit next to the airplane window.

7. "Do you have any liquids or electronics in your bag?"

Security checkpoint question regarding laptops, tablets, and bottled drinks.

8. "Do you have anything to declare?"

Customs question asking if you are carrying goods that require taxation.

9. "No, I have nothing to declare."

Standard response when carrying only personal items within duty-free limits.

10. "Where can I pick up my checked baggage?"

Inquiry asked at arrival to find the luggage carousel or belt.
