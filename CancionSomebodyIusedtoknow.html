📐 Grammar Focus: The "Used to" Family

📖 Cambridge Dictionary ↗

Students often confuse these three structures. Let's break them down clearly:

1. Used to + Infinitive

Use: For past habits or states that are no longer true now.

"We used to talk every single day."

2. Be used to + -ing / Noun

Use: To be accustomed to something; it's normal for you.

"Now she is used to living without him."

3. Get used to + -ing / Noun

Use: The process of becoming accustomed to something new.

"It takes time to get used to being single again."

💡 Key Expressions & British Pronunciation (UK)

Listen to the native British audio, study the definitions, and review practical examples.

1. "Somebody that I used to know"

Meaning: A person you had an intimate relationship with in the past, but now they are a total stranger.

Example: "We shared an apartment for years, but now we don't speak; she's just somebody that I used to know."

2. "Cut somebody out"

Meaning: To stop communicating with someone completely or remove them from your life.

Example: "After constant arguments, he decided to cut her out of his social media and phone contacts."

3. "Treat your data like you had no change"

Meaning: Acting coldly, pretending nothing happened, or treating an ex-partner as if they never mattered.

Example: "I said hello on the street, but she looked right through me and treated me like a total stranger."

4. "Stoop low"

Meaning: Behaving in a petty, mean, or ungenerous way that is beneath one's dignity.

Example: "He didn't need to spread rumors about her friends; it was unnecessary to stoop so low."

5. "Records"

Meaning: Vinyl music albums stored in sleeves, representing shared belongings divided up after the breakup.

Example: "When they moved out, their friends came over and collected all their records."

👤 About the Author: Gotye (Wally De Backer)

Read and listen to the biography of the artist behind the masterpiece.

📖 Dictionary

Wally De Backer, better known by his stage name Gotye, is a Belgian-Australian singer-songwriter and multi-instrumentalist born in Bruges, Belgium, in 1980, before moving to Australia as a child. His artistic name is a phonetic spelling of "Gautier", the French equivalent of his given name, Walter.

Gotye gained worldwide massive acclaim with his 2011 smash hit "Somebody That I Used to Know", featuring New Zealand singer Kimbra. The song topped charts in over 30 countries, became one of the best-selling digital singles of all time, and won two Grammy Awards, including Record of the Year.

Despite unprecedented global success, Gotye chose to step away from mainstream pop stardom. Rather than capitalizing on the fame with a rapid commercial follow-up, he chose to focus on independent music projects, archival art, and playing drums in his band The Basics. His thoughtful approach highlights a rare dedication to artistic integrity over commercial pressure.
