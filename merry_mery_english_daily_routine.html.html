📖 Mastering Daily Routines & Time Expressions

When discussing daily habits and everyday schedules, we rely on precise time expressions to clarify exact moments, general periods of the day, frequency, or chronological sequence.

💡 Key Grammar Essentials

• Times of day: in the morning, in the afternoon, in the evening, at night
• Specific times: at 7 o'clock, at half past seven, at quarter past eight
• Frequency: every day, usually, always, sometimes, never, on weekdays
• Sequence: first, then, next, after that, finally

Example Sentences

  • On weekdays, I get up early in the morning.
  • I get dressed at half past seven and then I have breakfast.
  • I go to work at a quarter past eight.
  • I have lunch with my coworkers at 12:30 p.m.
  • I finish work at 6:00 p.m. and go home.
  • After that, I usually relax and watch TV.
  • I go to bed at 10:00 p.m.

Present Simple Tense for Routines

We use the present simple tense for regular habits. Remember that third-person singular pronouns (he, she, it) require adding -s or -es to the main verb.

  • I wake up at 7:00 a.m. / She wakes up at 7:00 a.m.
  • My daughter goes to school every single day.
